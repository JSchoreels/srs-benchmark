# FSRS7-Instant: start of day and where the gain comes from, users 1-300

`fsrs_instant.py` on the cached per-fold benchmark fits (`runs/cache`), configs `runs/sod.json`,
`python fsrs_instant_compare.py runs/sod-300`. 300 users, 9.76M test reviews. Paired
user-bootstrap 95% CIs on the difference vs FSRS-7 (0.3519 per user, 0.3245 review-weighted).

Parts of the online update (settings as in the PR, not re-tuned):
- **full**: FSRS7-Instant (parameter step lr 1e-3 + user bias 0.1 + Again weight 0.01);
- **params-only**: the Adam parameter step alone;
- **bias-again-only**: user bias and Again-count weight alone, FSRS-7 parameters fixed.

Each scored at three times:
- **review**: just before the review (the PR's FSRS7-Instant number);
- **scheduling**: right after the card's previous review (fixed due date);
- **start of day**: before the user's first review of the day (a queue recomputed each morning).

LogLoss vs FSRS-7, per user:

| | review | start of day | scheduling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| full | -10.1% [-0.0420, -0.0297] | -2.3% [-0.0117, -0.0047] | +3.1% [+0.0074, +0.0151] |
| params-only | -7.1% [-0.0291, -0.0212] | **-3.8%** [-0.0160, -0.0107] | +0.4% [-0.0008, +0.0042] |
| bias-again-only | -8.4% [-0.0354, -0.0246] | -1.6% [-0.0088, -0.0028] | +3.1% [+0.0079, +0.0144] |

Review-weighted:

| | review | start of day | scheduling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| full | -8.6% | -2.0% [-0.0115, -0.0015] | +1.7% [+0.0013, +0.0096] |
| params-only | -7.0% | **-4.2%** [-0.0179, -0.0101] | -0.8% [-0.0056, +0.0003] |
| bias-again-only | -6.4% | -0.2% [-0.0045, +0.0041] | +2.7% [+0.0051, +0.0132] |

RMSE(bins) at start of day: full -26%, params-only -21%, bias-again-only -17%.
Review and scheduling rows are identical to `runs/quick-300` (max per-user diff 0).

Reading:
- At review time most of the gain is the user bias + Again weight alone (-8.4% of -10.1%):
  the moving-average-like part.
- That part is what makes scheduling worse (+3.1%); the parameter steps alone are neutral at
  scheduling time (+0.4%, CI across 0).
- For a queue recomputed each morning, the parameter steps alone are the best variant
  (-3.8% per user, -4.2% review-weighted); adding the bias makes it worse (-2.3%).
- Settings were chosen for review time on the dev users; start of day was not tuned.

Fix in this commit: the per-user driver never filled in the Anki day, so its start-of-day
mode saw day 0 for every review. Only the 5k shared-fit driver (`fsrs_instant_shared.py`) had
days, so the published -1.9% start-of-day number was not affected.

Wall time 25 min at 6 processes (cached fits; replay ~52 us per review per config, 9 configs).
